  case identifiers cannot establish patient identity.
- What is the team’s minimum clinically acceptable sensitivity before optimizing
  F1?

  by the local evaluator?

  the official baseline, and does it cover participant modification, Docker
  packaging, evaluation, publication, and source-code release?
- Which independently licensed or public-domain clinical knowledge sources, if
  any, are worth evaluating after the license-clean baseline is stable?
